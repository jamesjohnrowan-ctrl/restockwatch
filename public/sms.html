Restock alerts by SMS

Send one text when a retailer first reports a tracked console or controller version in stock. Each alert names the item and version. Nintendo metadata changing to InStock also sends a POSSIBLE STOCK alert so you can check the page yourself. This is unconfirmed availability. Repeated positive metadata readings, blocked checks and sellouts do not send texts. No historical alerts are sent when you turn this on.

Find these in your Twilio Console. Saved only in the local server’s data folder. Existing credentials never appear in the browser; leave blank to keep saved values.Include +1 for Canadian numbers. On a trial account, the destination must be verified in Twilio. Your sender must be approved for messaging.This is the tracker’s limit, not a Twilio credit or billing guarantee. A text can use multiple billable SMS segments. Confirm your trial allowance and expiry in Twilio Console.

Save and enable before testing. “Accepted” means Twilio accepted the request, not confirmed handset delivery. Check your phone and Twilio message logs. Failed or uncertain sends are not retried automatically.
